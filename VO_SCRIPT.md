## How to use this script

- The video's **visual timeline never shows the spoken words on screen** — this
  timeline is designed to be re-used for **Hindi, English and Bengali** voiceovers.
  Record each language against these same timestamps.
- Timestamps below are **scene boundaries**. Each block should comfortably fit its
  window at a natural, unhurried pace (~2.5–2.7 words/second). There is deliberate
  breathing room; do not rush.
- Suggested ElevenLabs settings: a warm, confident male or female voice;
  **Stability 45–55, Similarity 75–85, Style 0–15**, speed ~1.0. Leave ~0.3s of
  silence at the start so the first line lands just after the opening whoosh.
- Tone: premium, calm, assured — a high-end studio brand, not a hard-sell ad.
- Pronunciation: **Neumann** = "NOY-mahn". **TLM 107** = "T-L-M one-oh-seven".
  **₹1,44,900** = "one lakh forty-four thousand nine hundred rupees".
